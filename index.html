Complete Step-by-Step Flashing Procedure

⚠️ CRITICAL PRE-REQUISITE (eSIM vs External Physical SIM)

If your 4G USB stick is on stock Android and has an eSIM + External Physical SIM slot, you MUST log into the stock web admin panel (usually 192.168.100.1 or 192.168.0.1), navigate to network settings, and select "External SIM" as Default before flashing. If you flash without doing this, the modem baseband may remain switched to the internal eSIM and fail to detect your physical SIM card.

Prerequisites & Tools

Make sure you have installed on your computer:

  • EDL Tool: bkerler/edl (Qualcomm Sahara/Firehose dumper)
  • Android SDK Platform-Tools: fastboot and adb
  • Proper Qualcomm 9008 USB drivers (Linux has native qcserial support)

Step 1: Backup Critical Device Partitions via EDL

Place your 4G USB dongle in EDL (Emergency Download 9008) mode (via test points or EDL cable), then backup calibration & IMEI partitions:

for n in fsc fsg modem modemst1 modemst2 persist sec; do edl r ${n} ${n}.bin done

Step 2: Flash Aboot Bootloader & Enter Fastboot

Write the unlocked aboot.mbn and reboot into fastboot:

edl w aboot aboot.mbn edl e boot edl reset

Step 3: Flash OpenWrt Firmware Partitions

Execute the following commands in Fastboot mode to flash the GPT partition layout and firmware components:

fastboot flash partition gpt_both0.bin fastboot flash aboot aboot.mbn fastboot flash hyp hyp.mbn fastboot flash rpm rpm.mbn fastboot flash sbl1 sbl1.mbn fastboot flash tz tz.mbn fastboot flash boot boot.bin fastboot flash rootfs rootfs.bin

Step 4: Restore Original Modem & Calibration Partitions

Restore your original IMEI, MAC address, and NVRAM calibrations:

for n in fsc fsg modem modemst1 modemst2 persist sec; do fastboot flash ${n} ${n}.bin done fastboot reboot

✓ Done! Your 4G dongle will now boot into OpenWrt Linux.
